# The World in One Day — news-image-01

One image that sums up the world's news of Friday 13 November 2026,
drawn as a newspaper front page: six panels, each with a pictogram, a
kicker, a headline and a short summary.

1. Europe joins the Ukraine peace talks; work begins on the war's
   largest prisoner exchange.
2. Nvidia chief Jensen Huang resigns; shares fall 16%.
3. Microsoft 365 goes dark: Outlook and Teams fail worldwide.
4. Four oil tankers struck in the Strait of Hormuz; Iran says it hit
   a US radar in Bahrain (claim unverified).
5. Brooklyn building collapse: eight dead, incl. two children.
6. US court backs seizure of $1.2B stolen from Binance in the hack
   the FBI blames on North Korea.

## Run

    python3 main.py

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, pure Python standard library, no
network, no randomness). Two runs write byte-identical files.
